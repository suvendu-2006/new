Frequently Asked Questions

How do I register for a tournament?

+

Simply fill out the registration form above with your gamer tag, select your preferred game and skill level, and hit "Register & Compete". You'll receive a confirmation email with all tournament details.

Is registration free?

+

Yes! All tournaments on NEXUS ARENA are completely free to enter. Just register and start competing with other players!

What games are available?

+

We currently host tournaments for Ludo, UNP, Stumble Guys, Among Us, and Free Fire. More games are being added regularly!

Can I play in a team?

+

Absolutely! Many tournaments support squad/team entries. Register your team leader first, then invite squad members through the team invite link sent to your email. Solo players can also use our matchmaking feature.

What platforms are supported?

+

We support PC, Mobile (Android & iOS), and Console gaming. Each tournament specifies the supported platform. Cross-platform tournaments are also available for select games.
